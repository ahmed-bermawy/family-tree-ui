🌳 Family Tree Maker — Build, Share & Explore Your Family History

Create your family tree online for free. Add parents, children, siblings and spouses, upload photos, and share your family history with relatives through a private link. The whole app works in English and Arabic.

What you can do

  • Build your tree visually — add relatives and see the graph grow with automatic layout.
  • Organize & edit — update names, genders and photos at any time.
  • Polygamous families — a man can be connected to more than one wife, and each wife keeps her own children beneath her.
  • Share with family — every tree has a private link; relatives open it read-only without an account.
  • Export & print — download your tree to keep or print it.
  • Two languages — full English and Arabic interface with right-to-left support.
  • Birth & death dates — record a birth date and a death date for anyone, and choose what shows: the full date, the year only, or keep it private. Deceased relatives are gently marked with a dove.
  • Birthday reminders — a badge lists every family birthday in the next 30 days, with the days remaining and the age they turn, plus a one-tap WhatsApp wish.
  • Arrange it your way — siblings order themselves by birth date (eldest first, on the side you choose), and you can drag anyone to a spot of your own; the arrangement is saved.
  • Try before signing up — start building a tree straight from the home page with no account, and keep it when you register.

Frequently asked questions

Is the family tree maker free?

Yes. Creating a family tree is completely free — build your tree, add relatives and photos, and share it with a private link at no cost.

Can I build a family tree with more than one wife?

Yes. The app is built for real family structures: a man can be connected to more than one wife, each wife keeps her own children beneath her, and every child stays linked to both parents.

Do relatives need an account to view my tree?

No. Every tree has a private share link. Anyone with the link can open and explore it in read-only mode without registering.

Can I add birth and death dates to my family tree?

Yes. Every person can have a birth date and a death date, each with its own display setting: show the full date, show the year only, or keep it private. Private dates are stored but never displayed, and deceased relatives are marked with a dove so they are easy to recognise on the tree.

Does the family tree app remind me of birthdays?

Yes. A badge on the tree shows every birthday falling in the next 30 days, how many days are left, and the age the person is turning. From the same list you can send a birthday wish on WhatsApp in one tap.

Can I arrange the family tree in my own order?

Yes. Siblings are ordered automatically by birth date with the eldest first, and you can choose whether the eldest sits on the right or the left. You can also drag any person to wherever you want them — the position is saved and stays after a refresh, with one click to return to the automatic layout.

Can I use the family tree app in Arabic?

Yes. The whole interface is available in English and Arabic, with full right-to-left (RTL) support.

Can I add photos to the people in my tree?

Yes. You can upload a profile photo for every person in the tree, along with their name and gender.
